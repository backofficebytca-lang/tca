## How they were chosen

The brief asked for AI-generated campaign images through the fal.ai connection. Every generation
request returned `Forbidden` (both `nano-banana-pro` and `flux-2`), so the images were instead selected
from licensed photography: warm oak-and-paper desk scenes, golden-hour Parisian facades, archive
shelving and glass interiors, so that they read as one campaign. No colour grading is applied;
they are used as photographed. Rules applied: no faces, no logos, no fake documents.
Where a printed form is visible it is a real, unreadable-at-size photograph of generic stationery.

Photos are served through `next/image` (AVIF/WebP). Masters are stored as progressive JPEG.
